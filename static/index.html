Automatic ListenBrainz Sync (Official Export API)

Token: Not set

Triggers api.listenbrainz.org/1/export/ (the exact same archive as listenbrainz.org/settings/export/), downloads the .zip into imports/, extracts it, and updates your dashboard. Copy your User Token from listenbrainz.org/settings/ (no password or cookies needed).

Manual Export Alternative

  1. Open listenbrainz.org/settings/export/ while signed in.
  2. Click Export listens under the listens section and download your listenbrainz_<username>_<id>.zip archive.
  3. Drop that .zip (or extracted folder) into this project's imports/ directory, or upload/select it below.
